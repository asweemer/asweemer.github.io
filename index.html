What I think about

The capability curve is near vertical. The judgment curve is flat.

That gap is most of what I write about, and most of what I build a business around.

The magic needs a magician.

AI gets better fastest where the output can be scored. Nobody has a clean training signal for "should you have deleted that database." Capability compounds. Judgment doesn't, yet. The humans who know when the machine is wrong are the ones you keep.

Refactor the driver out of the engine.

Every system has a Driver and an Engine. The Engine is deterministic code that does the work safely, at scale, and never improvises. The Driver is the reasoning layer that reads intent and decides which engine to start, and when. For decades we built both into one box and called it automation. AI doesn't replace the Engine. It finally gives us a real Driver. The AI thinks. The code executes. Neither does the other's job.

You can't fire an API.

In managed services, accountability is the product. When the agent is wrong at 3 a.m., somebody with a name answers the phone. That's what customers are actually buying, and it's the part AI makes more valuable, not less.
